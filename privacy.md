# Privacy Policy / 隐私声明

## English

**ConnSSH** respects your privacy.

This application **does not collect, store, or share any personal data**.

### Data Collection
ConnSSH does **not collect any of the following:**
- Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Device identifiers
- Usage analytics
- Location data
- SSH credentials or connection data

### Data Usage
All data (such as SSH connection information) is:
- Stored **locally on your device only**
- **Never transmitted** to any server
- Fully controlled by the user

### Permissions
If the app requests system permissions (e.g., Photo Library access), they are:
- Used only for specific features (if applicable)
- **Not used for tracking or data collection**

### Third-party Services
ConnSSH **does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs**.

### Security
Your data remains on your device. We do not have access to it.

### Contact
If you have any questions, please contact by samuioto@outlook.com
